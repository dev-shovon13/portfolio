# Product: Kawser Ahmed Shovon, portfolio

## What it is
The personal site of Kawser Ahmed Shovon, a software engineer in Dhaka. Always call him a software engineer, never a "frontend engineer". He has 5+ years of experience (Dec 2021 to present) with React.js, Next.js and TypeScript.

## Who arrives
A general professional audience: recruiters, hiring managers, engineers he has worked with, and potential collaborators. Most arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub or a resume link, on a laptop, scanning for about a minute before deciding whether to read on.

## What must be true after a visit
- He builds data-heavy interfaces for systems where correctness matters:
  - NeuString, Yaana Technologies' wholesale telecom roaming platform. He owns the Reconciliation, Billing & Charging Evaluation (BCE) and Amazon QuickSight (AQS) modules.
  - A Bangladesh national election management system.
  - The Anti-Corruption Commission (ACC) ERP.
  - The National Intelligence Platform (NIP), a government telecom monitoring system.
- He works with distributed teams in India, Ukraine, Poland and the USA, reviews code and mentors junior developers across all Penta projects.
- He also builds backends on personal projects: Node.js and Express REST APIs over MongoDB. Endpoints are shown from the public server repos.
- Off the clock he travels: 48 of Bangladesh's 64 districts, plus India (Kolkata, Agra, Delhi, Manali), Singapore and Malaysia (Johor Bahru, Malacca, Kuala Lumpur, Genting Highlands, Penang, Langkawi).
- The visitor can reach the resume, email, LinkedIn and GitHub within seconds, and can build a tailored CV at /cv.

## Constraints
- All professional work is under NDA. Show no real screenshots, client data or invented metrics. Recreated UI and demo data must be labeled as illustrative.
- The intelligence project gets minimal wording.
- Earlier public work (Corify, Traveezy, Helping Hand) can be linked.

## Brand commitments
- Continuity with the first portfolio: a deep navy ground (`#0a192f`) and a mint signal (`#62f9d5`). Mint means "reconciled / matched".
- Colorful, but no coral or orange. Yellow (`#ffd23f`) is the lead accent.
- No photos of him on the site.
- The tone is precise, calm and confident. No hype, no "passionate developer" filler.
